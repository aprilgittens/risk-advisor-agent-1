# Tailspin Toys

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** Tailspin Toys  
**Industry:** Consumer Products and Toy Manufacturing  
**Headquarters:** Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States  
**Founded:** 1994  
**Employees:** 6,200  
**Annual Revenue:** $2.4 billion  
**Primary Markets:** United States, Canada, Mexico, and Europe  

Tailspin Toys designs, manufactures, licenses, and distributes children's toys, games, collectibles, and electronic entertainment products.

The company's product portfolio includes traditional toys, educational products, battery-powered toys, connected toys, and licensed merchandise.

Revenue is highly seasonal, with approximately 46% of annual sales occurring during the fourth quarter.

## Supply Chain

Tailspin Toys has a globally distributed supply chain involving:

- Plastics
- Electronic components
- Packaging materials
- Batteries
- Textiles
- Licensed product components

Approximately 52% of annual product volume is produced by third-party manufacturers.

Several top-selling products depend on a small number of specialized suppliers.

Key supply chain concerns include:

- Seasonal production deadlines
- Ocean freight dependency
- Port congestion
- Supplier quality
- Product safety requirements
- Geographic concentration
- Limited time to recover from delays before holiday sales periods

Tailspin Toys typically begins peak holiday production six to eight months before the selling season.

## Product Safety and Liability

Product safety is one of Tailspin Toys' most significant risks.

The company sells products intended for children ranging from infants to teenagers.

Risk considerations include:

- Choking hazards
- Battery safety
- Small components
- Chemical exposure
- Product labeling
- Age grading
- Connected-device privacy
- Manufacturing defects

Tailspin Toys maintains internal product testing teams and also uses independent laboratories.

All new products undergo safety testing before commercial release.

## Connected Toys and Cyber Risk

Approximately 14% of Tailspin Toys' annual revenue now comes from connected or app-enabled products.

Connected products may include:

- Bluetooth connectivity
- Mobile applications
- Cloud accounts
- Voice features
- User profiles
- Parental controls

Current cybersecurity controls include:

- Secure software development practices
- Encryption for customer account data

- Annual penetration testing
- Privacy reviews for connected products

Known concerns include:

- Third-party mobile SDKs
- Children's privacy requirements
- Legacy connected products
- Cloud service dependency

## Property and Business Interruption Exposure

Tailspin Toys owns approximately $780 million in physical property, inventory, equipment, and distribution assets.

Key operational exposures include:

- Seasonal inventory concentration
- Distribution center dependency
- Warehouse fire
- Severe weather
- Transportation interruption
- Manufacturing shutdown
- Product recall events

The Dallas distribution center handles approximately 31% of U.S. holiday-season shipments.

A prolonged outage during the fourth quarter could significantly affect annual revenue.

## Claims History

### Product Recall

**2025 — Battery-Powered Toy Recall**

Tailspin Toys recalled approximately 420,000 units after receiving reports of battery overheating.

Estimated total cost: **$14.5 million**

Primary cost drivers:

- Product replacement
- Retailer reimbursement
- Logistics
- Customer communication
- Legal expenses

### Property

**2024 — Dallas Distribution Center**

A severe storm damaged part of the warehouse roof and interrupted operations for four days.

Estimated total loss: **$5.7 million**

### Supply Chain

**2023 — Port Disruption**

Shipping delays affected inventory availability for several holiday product lines.

Estimated financial impact: **$8.9 million**

### Cyber

**2022 — Customer Account Incident**

Credential-stuffing activity affected a small percentage of customer accounts associated with a connected-toy application.

Estimated response cost: **$1.3 million**
